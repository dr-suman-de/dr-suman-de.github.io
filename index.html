About Dr. Suman De

Who I Am

A results-driven professional with 11 years at SAP and a PhD from IIT Kharagpur’s Centre for Computational & Data Sciences, Dr. Suman De leads Research & Industry-Academia Partnerships at SAP Labs India. His work spans across enterprise product management, cutting-edge AI, Cloud ERP, Digital Twin research, and academia — co-developing curricula Business Schools, IITs, and other leading institutions.

With 52 research publications across IEEE Transactions on Artificial Intelligence, IEEE Engineering Management Review, and Springer, 282+ citations (h-index: 10), and 11 international keynote talks, Dr. De is a recognised thought leader. He is a former Visiting Faculty for MBA students at Symbiosis International University and was nominated for the Indian Youth Engineering Icon of the Year at the IET Awards in 2024.
