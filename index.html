Who am I?

Head of Research & Industry-Academia Partnerships at SAP Labs India

Dr. Suman De is a researcher, author, and thought leader in Cloud ERP, Industry 4.0, and Software Engineering. With over 280 citations and an h-index of 10, he actively bridges academia and enterprise technology. He is co-author of Haphazard Reality Revisited: Half a Century of Random Thoughts with Shoumen Palit Austin Datta (MIT), and a keynote speaker at IEEE conferences worldwide.
